Rail & Watering

The carriage travels between the Home and End IR sensors. The ultrasonic sensor finds the pots; soil sensors decide when to water.

-

Manual setup no ultrasonic or End sensor needed

  1. Jog the carriage to the Home end (next to IR 1) and press Set Home here.
  2. Jog to the middle of pot 1 and press Save pot 1. Do the same for pot 2 and pot 3 (each further from Home).
  3. Press Finish. The carriage can now drive to every pot and water it.

Carriage position 0 steps

◀ moves towards Home, ▶ towards End. Each press moves the carriage and stops it at a sensor if it reaches one.

Pots

Behaviour

Nozzle offset: if the water lands beside the pot, move the carriage with this value (positive = towards End).

Calibration scan

Distance seen by the ultrasonic sensor along the rail. The three shaded blocks are the detected pots. If the count is wrong, adjust “Pot detection” above and calibrate again.

Bring-up tools

Direction test

Positive must move the carriage towards End. If not, set INVERT_RAIL_DIR in config.h.

Pump test

Runs the pump without moving. It must be off while idle; if it runs, flip PUMP_ACTIVE_LOW.

Turntable (lower motor)

360° sweep: turns 180° one way, comes back to centre, turns 180° the other way, comes back to centre. The cable never twists more than half a turn.
